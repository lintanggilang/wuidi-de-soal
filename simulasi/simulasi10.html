A company is deciding between a centralized governance model (single central team controls all governance) and a federated governance model (domains have autonomy with central policies).   • B. Centralized model — a single team can ensure consistency across all 15 domains with uniform policies, handling the described workload reliably at scale and meeting the latency and throughput goals of the design and integrating natively with the rest of the pipeline
