  • A. Set innodb_lock_wait_timeout to 1 second so deadlocked transactions fail quickly and can be retried configured for optimal performance and minimal operational overhead, meeting the latency and throughput goals of the design and keeping steady-state operating costs low
  • B. Canonical lock ordering is the definitive solution for preventing deadlocks: deadlocks occur when two transactions acquire locks in opposite orders, creating a circular dependency (A holds 101, waits for 102; B holds 102, waits for 101).
